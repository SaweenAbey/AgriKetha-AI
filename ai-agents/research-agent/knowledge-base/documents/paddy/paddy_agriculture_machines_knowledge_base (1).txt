PADDY FIELD MACHINERY KNOWLEDGE BASE

Purpose

This knowledge base provides practical information about machines
commonly used in paddy (rice) cultivation. It explains what each machine
is used for, basic operating steps, suitable use cases, and important
safety precautions.

IMPORTANT SAFETY NOTE

Machine operation should always follow the manufacturer’s manual and the
machine’s safety labels. Farmers should receive proper training before
operating powered machinery. Never bypass guards or safety switches.
Stop the engine, isolate the power source, and wait for moving parts to
stop before clearing blockages, adjusting attachments, or performing
maintenance.

1. TRACTOR

What it is used for: - Pulling and operating agricultural implements. -
Primary and secondary land preparation. - Ploughing, puddling,
harrowing, leveling, and transporting materials. - Operating compatible
pumps and other PTO-driven equipment.

Common paddy uses: - Pulling a mouldboard/disc plough. - Pulling a
rotavator. - Pulling a puddler. - Pulling a harrow. - Operating a
trailer. - Operating a compatible land-leveling implement.

How to use: 1. Inspect engine oil, coolant, fuel, tyres, brakes,
steering, hydraulic system, PTO, and hitch. 2. Select an implement
suitable for the field operation. 3. Attach the implement securely using
the correct hitch and locking mechanism. 4. Keep bystanders away during
attachment and operation. 5. Start the tractor according to the
manufacturer’s procedure. 6. Select a suitable gear and engine speed for
the implement and field condition. 7. Operate at a controlled speed. 8.
Raise or disengage the implement before turning or transporting it when
required. 9. Disengage the PTO before leaving the operator position. 10.
Park on stable ground, lower implements, apply the parking brake, and
stop the engine.

Safety cautions: - Never allow untrained people or children to operate
the tractor. - Use the seat belt where a ROPS (roll-over protective
structure) is fitted. - Avoid steep slopes and unstable or excessively
wet ground. - Never carry passengers unless the tractor is specifically
designed for them. - Keep away from PTO shafts and rotating parts. -
Never inspect or repair an attachment while it is running. - Use proper
ballast and implement settings to maintain stability.

2. POWER TILLER / WALK-BEHIND TILLER

What it is used for: - Small and medium field land preparation. -
Ploughing, puddling, harrowing, and soil mixing. - Useful where a
full-size tractor is difficult to use.

How to use: 1. Check engine oil, fuel, transmission oil, tyres/wheels,
controls, and attachment. 2. Start the engine in a clear area. 3. Select
the appropriate gear and operating depth. 4. Guide the machine steadily
across the field. 5. Avoid excessive speed. 6. Stop the machine before
changing attachments or making adjustments. 7. Park securely and shut
down the engine after work.

Safety cautions: - Keep feet away from rotary blades. - Never remove
guards while the machine is running. - Do not reverse toward people. -
Be especially careful in muddy fields and near field edges. - Stop the
engine before clearing mud or plant material. - Wear suitable footwear
and avoid loose clothing.

3. MOULDBOARD / DISC PLOUGH

What it is used for: - Initial soil turning and breaking. -
Incorporating crop residues and weeds. - Preparing soil before puddling
where appropriate.

How to use: 1. Attach the plough to the tractor’s three-point linkage.
2. Adjust working depth according to soil condition. 3. Start with a
shallow test pass. 4. Adjust the implement for consistent soil turning.
5. Maintain a controlled speed and overlap passes appropriately.

Safety cautions: - Never stand behind or beside a suspended implement
during attachment. - Lower the implement before inspection. - Do not
make adjustments while the tractor or implement is moving. - Keep people
away from the operating area.

4. ROTAVATOR / ROTARY TILLER

What it is used for: - Breaking and mixing soil. - Preparing the
seedbed. - Mixing crop residues into soil. - In some systems, preparing
soil before puddling.

How to use: 1. Connect the rotavator to the tractor. 2. Check blades,
guards, gearbox oil, PTO shaft, and safety shield. 3. Set the working
depth. 4. Engage PTO at the recommended speed. 5. Operate at the
manufacturer’s recommended forward speed. 6. Make overlapping passes as
needed. 7. Disengage PTO before lifting, turning, stopping, or leaving
the tractor when required.

Safety cautions: - The rotary blades can cause severe injury. - Never
approach the blades while they are rotating. - Never clear wrapped
vegetation with hands while the PTO is engaged. - Stop the engine and
isolate the PTO before clearing blockages. - Ensure the PTO shaft guard
is installed.

5. PUDDLER

What it is used for: - Puddling flooded soil before transplanting
rice. - Breaking soil clods. - Mixing soil and water to create a
suitable puddled layer. - Reducing water movement through the soil when
used appropriately.

How to use: 1. Flood the field according to the local cultivation
practice. 2. Operate the puddler at an appropriate soil and water
condition. 3. Make passes across the field to obtain uniform puddling.
4. Avoid unnecessary repeated passes that can damage soil structure or
waste fuel. 5. Level the field after puddling when required.

Safety cautions: - Mud can reduce tractor stability and traction. - Keep
away from deep holes, bunds, drains, and unstable field edges. - Do not
operate beyond the machine’s safe working conditions. - Use appropriate
tractor ballast and tyres.

6. HARROW

What it is used for: - Breaking clods. - Smoothing and leveling the soil
surface. - Preparing the seedbed after ploughing.

How to use: 1. Attach the harrow securely. 2. Set the working depth and
angle if adjustable. 3. Operate at a suitable speed. 4. Make systematic
passes to obtain an even surface.

Safety cautions: - Keep people away from the implement. - Lower the
harrow before inspection. - Stop the tractor before making adjustments.

7. LASER LAND LEVELER / LAND LEVELER

What it is used for: - Producing a more uniform field surface. -
Improving distribution of irrigation water. - Reducing areas of
excessive or insufficient water depth. - Supporting more uniform crop
establishment.

How to use: 1. Survey or identify high and low areas. 2. Attach the
leveling system correctly. 3. Set the laser transmitter and receiver
according to the equipment manual when using a laser system. 4. Move
soil from high areas toward low areas. 5. Make repeated passes until the
desired level is achieved. 6. Check the field before cultivation.

Safety cautions: - Never look directly into a laser beam. - Keep
unauthorized people away from laser equipment. - Follow the laser
equipment safety instructions. - Avoid operating heavy equipment close
to unstable bunds or drains.

8. RICE SEED DRILL / SEEDER

What it is used for: - Mechanized sowing of rice seed. - Placing seed at
relatively controlled spacing and depth depending on the machine.

How to use: 1. Prepare and level the field appropriately for the seeding
system. 2. Select clean, suitable seed. 3. Fill the seed hopper. 4.
Calibrate the seed rate before field operation. 5. Set the desired row
spacing and depth if adjustable. 6. Operate at the recommended speed. 7.
Check seed placement periodically.

Safety cautions: - Stop the machine before removing blocked seed
tubes. - Keep hands away from moving metering mechanisms. - Do not enter
the machine’s working path while operating. - Use only the recommended
seed type and settings.

9. RICE TRANSPLANTER

What it is used for: - Mechanized transplanting of rice seedlings. -
Produces relatively uniform row and plant spacing.

How to use: 1. Raise healthy seedlings using the nursery method required
by the transplanter. 2. Prepare seedling mats or trays according to the
machine specification. 3. Prepare and level the paddy field. 4. Load
seedling mats/trays correctly. 5. Adjust planting depth and row spacing
according to the machine. 6. Start slowly and verify that seedlings are
being planted correctly. 7. Refill seedlings when necessary. 8. Check
planted rows periodically.

Safety cautions: - Keep hands away from planting mechanisms while
operating. - Stop the engine before servicing or clearing a jam. - Do
not allow people to stand in the machine’s operating path. - Avoid
excessively deep or uneven field conditions outside the machine’s
operating range.

10. DIRECT SEEDER / BROADCAST SEEDER

What it is used for: - Direct seeding of rice into the field. - Reduces
the need for manual transplanting when conditions and production systems
are suitable.

How to use: 1. Prepare the field according to the direct-seeding method.
2. Use suitable seed and recommended seed rate. 3. Calibrate the
machine. 4. Operate at a controlled speed. 5. Check seed distribution
regularly. 6. Maintain appropriate water management after seeding.

Safety cautions: - Stop the machine before clearing blocked seed
outlets. - Keep hands away from rotating metering mechanisms. - Use the
correct seed rate and machine settings.

11. RICE REAPER

What it is used for: - Cutting mature rice plants during harvesting. -
Useful for reducing manual harvesting labor.

How to use: 1. Harvest when the crop reaches the locally recommended
maturity stage. 2. Check cutting blades, guards, belts, fuel, and
controls. 3. Set the machine according to crop height and field
condition. 4. Move through the crop in an organized pattern. 5. Collect
or arrange the cut crop for threshing.

Safety cautions: - Keep hands away from cutting blades. - Stop the
engine before clearing wrapped straw. - Keep people away from the
cutting area. - Do not operate in conditions where the machine becomes
unstable.

12. COMBINE HARVESTER

What it is used for: - Cutting rice. - Feeding crop material into the
machine. - Threshing. - Separating grain from straw. - Cleaning
harvested grain. - Collecting grain, depending on machine design.

How to use: 1. Check fuel, engine oil, coolant, belts, chains, cutting
unit, guards, tyres/tracks, and grain tank. 2. Set the machine for the
rice variety and field condition. 3. Enter the field only when the crop
is at the appropriate harvesting stage. 4. Operate at the recommended
speed. 5. Monitor grain loss and grain cleanliness. 6. Adjust machine
settings according to the operator manual and crop condition. 7. Empty
the grain tank at safe locations. 8. Clean the machine after harvesting.

Safety cautions: - Never approach the header, auger, feeder, threshing
unit, or other moving components while running. - Shut down the engine
and isolate power before maintenance or blockage removal. - Never climb
onto moving machinery. - Keep people away from unloading operations. -
Take extra care around field edges, drains, bunds, and slopes. - Use
lighting and visibility equipment when operating in low-light
conditions.

13. RICE THRESHER

What it is used for: - Separating rice grain from harvested
panicles/straw.

How to use: 1. Position the thresher on stable, level ground. 2. Inspect
belts, pulleys, guards, bearings, and feeding mechanism. 3. Start the
machine according to the manufacturer’s instructions. 4. Feed harvested
rice steadily without overloading. 5. Collect separated grain and remove
straw safely. 6. Stop the machine and clean it after use.

Safety cautions: - Never put hands into the feeding or threshing
mechanism. - Keep all guards installed. - Do not wear loose clothing
near rotating shafts. - Stop and isolate the machine before removing
blocked material. - Keep children and bystanders away.

14. WINNOWER / GRAIN CLEANER

What it is used for: - Removing chaff, straw fragments, dust, and
lighter impurities from harvested rice. - Improving grain cleanliness
before storage or further processing.

How to use: 1. Dry or condition the grain as required. 2. Adjust airflow
or cleaning settings. 3. Feed grain at the recommended rate. 4. Check
the cleaned grain regularly. 5. Clean the machine after use.

Safety cautions: - Avoid inhaling dust. - Use appropriate respiratory
protection where dust levels are high. - Keep hands away from fans and
moving parts. - Disconnect power before cleaning.

15. GRAIN DRYER

What it is used for: - Reducing grain moisture after harvest. - Helping
prevent deterioration during storage when properly operated.

How to use: 1. Check the dryer, fuel/electrical system, fans,
temperature controls, and airflow. 2. Load grain according to the
machine capacity. 3. Set temperature and airflow according to the grain
and dryer manufacturer’s instructions. 4. Monitor grain moisture and
temperature. 5. Stop the system safely after the target moisture
condition is reached.

Safety cautions: - Excessive drying temperature can damage grain
quality. - Keep combustible materials away from heat sources. - Ensure
adequate ventilation. - Do not bypass temperature or safety controls. -
Clean dust and grain residue regularly because grain dust can present a
fire or explosion hazard.

16. WATER PUMP

What it is used for: - Irrigation. - Removing excess water from
fields. - Moving water from canals, wells, tanks, or other water
sources.

Types: - Electric pump. - Petrol/diesel engine pump. - Tractor
PTO-driven pump.

How to use: 1. Place the pump on a stable surface. 2. Connect suction
and discharge pipes correctly. 3. Ensure the intake is protected from
large debris. 4. Prime the pump when required by the pump design. 5.
Start the pump according to the manufacturer’s procedure. 6. Monitor
water flow and engine/pump temperature. 7. Stop the pump before moving
or servicing it.

Safety cautions: - Never handle electrical equipment with wet hands or
while standing in water. - Use proper electrical protection and
grounding for electric pumps. - Keep fuel away from hot engine parts. -
Never run a pump outside its specified operating conditions. - Stop the
engine before clearing a blocked intake.

17. KNAPSACK SPRAYER

What it is used for: - Applying approved pesticides, fungicides,
herbicides, or foliar products. - Applying products to weeds, pests, or
crop foliage according to the product label.

How to use: 1. Identify the problem correctly before selecting a
chemical. 2. Read the product label. 3. Wear the required personal
protective equipment (PPE). 4. Measure and mix only the recommended
amount. 5. Calibrate the sprayer to apply the required volume. 6. Spray
in suitable weather conditions. 7. Avoid spraying against strong wind.
8. Clean the sprayer safely after use.

Safety cautions: - Never mix chemicals without reading the label. -
Never exceed the recommended dose. - Do not spray into strong wind or
toward people, animals, houses, waterways, or food. - Do not eat, drink,
or smoke while handling chemicals. - Wash hands and exposed skin after
spraying. - Store chemicals securely in their original labeled
containers. - Dispose of containers according to local regulations. - Do
not use leaking sprayers.

18. TRACTOR-MOUNTED / POWER SPRAYER

What it is used for: - Spraying larger paddy fields. - Applying approved
crop-protection products over larger areas.

How to use: 1. Calibrate the sprayer. 2. Check nozzles, hoses, tank,
pump, pressure, and guards. 3. Fill and mix products according to the
label. 4. Operate at the calibrated speed and pressure. 5. Maintain
appropriate nozzle height and coverage. 6. Clean the equipment safely
after spraying.

Safety cautions: - Follow all chemical PPE and label requirements. -
Keep people and animals away from the spray area. - Never repair
pressurized components while the pump is running. - Do not spray in
unsuitable wind or weather conditions.

19. FERTILIZER BROADCASTER / FERTILIZER SPREADER

What it is used for: - Applying granular fertilizer uniformly across the
field.

How to use: 1. Identify the fertilizer and required application rate. 2.
Calibrate the spreader. 3. Fill the hopper without overloading. 4.
Operate at a consistent speed. 5. Maintain the correct spread width and
overlap. 6. Check application uniformity.

Safety cautions: - Wear gloves and appropriate protective equipment when
handling fertilizer. - Avoid inhaling fertilizer dust. - Wash hands
after handling. - Never enter or reach into a moving spreader. - Keep
fertilizer away from water sources unless the product and application
plan specifically allow it.

20. MECHANICAL WEEDER / CONO WEEDER / ROTARY WEEDER

What it is used for: - Controlling weeds between rice rows. - Aerating
or disturbing the soil surface in suitable rice production systems.

How to use: 1. Use when the crop and soil conditions are suitable for
mechanical weeding. 2. Set the weeder according to row spacing. 3. Move
carefully between rows. 4. Avoid damaging rice plants. 5. Clean the
machine after use.

Safety cautions: - Keep hands away from rotating parts. - Stop the
machine before clearing weeds. - Do not use equipment in conditions
where it causes excessive crop damage.

21. TRACTOR TRAILER

What it is used for: - Transporting seed, fertilizer, harvested rice,
straw, tools, and other agricultural materials.

How to use: 1. Check trailer tyres, brakes, hitch, lights, and load
capacity. 2. Connect the trailer securely. 3. Distribute the load
evenly. 4. Secure loose materials. 5. Drive slowly, especially on wet
fields and roads. 6. Unload on stable ground.

Safety cautions: - Never exceed the rated load capacity. - Do not
transport people unless the trailer is specifically designed and
approved for passengers. - Reduce speed when turning. - Ensure the hitch
is locked correctly. - Keep the load stable.

22. AGRICULTURAL DRONE

What it is used for: - Field observation and crop monitoring. - Mapping
and collecting aerial images. - In approved systems, applying certain
agricultural products.

How to use: 1. Check local aviation and agricultural regulations. 2.
Inspect propellers, batteries, motors, tank, nozzles, and controls. 3.
Plan the flight area. 4. Keep people and obstacles outside the operating
area. 5. Calibrate the drone as required. 6. Fly according to the
manufacturer’s limits. 7. For spraying, use only products and
application methods permitted by the relevant label and regulations. 8.
Clean and store the drone after use.

Safety cautions: - Never fly near people without appropriate safety
controls. - Keep clear of power lines and trees. - Do not fly in weather
beyond the manufacturer’s limits. - Follow local drone regulations. -
Never touch rotating propellers. - Handle batteries carefully and charge
them using approved equipment. - For spraying, use appropriate PPE and
avoid drift toward people, animals, homes, and water.

23. RICE MILLING MACHINE

What it is used for: - Removing husk from paddy. - Producing brown rice
and/or milled white rice depending on the machine. - Some systems also
polish and grade rice.

How to use: 1. Clean and dry paddy to the required condition. 2. Inspect
the machine and safety guards. 3. Set the machine according to the paddy
and required output. 4. Feed grain at the recommended rate. 5. Monitor
output quality. 6. Stop and clean the machine after processing.

Safety cautions: - Keep hands away from rollers and moving parts. -
Control dust accumulation. - Use suitable dust protection and
ventilation. - Disconnect power before maintenance.

24. GRAIN BAGGING / WEIGHING MACHINE

What it is used for: - Measuring and packing harvested rice or grain
into bags.

How to use: 1. Set the target weight. 2. Calibrate the weighing system.
3. Position the bag securely. 4. Fill to the target weight. 5. Seal and
label the bag. 6. Store bags in a clean, dry location.

Safety cautions: - Avoid lifting heavy bags manually when mechanical
handling is available. - Keep fingers away from sealing and moving
mechanisms. - Maintain a clean working area to reduce slipping and
contamination.

GENERAL MACHINE SAFETY FOR PADDY FARMERS

Before operating: - Read the operator manual. - Check fuel, oil,
coolant, tyres, belts, chains, guards, brakes, steering, and hydraulic
systems. - Check that all safety guards are installed. - Inspect the
field for people, animals, large stones, deep holes, drains, and other
obstacles. - Wear suitable footwear and clothing. - Use the required
PPE.

During operation: - Operate only machines you are trained to use. - Keep
children and bystanders away. - Never wear loose clothing around
rotating machinery. - Never put hands into moving mechanisms. - Do not
exceed the machine’s rated capacity. - Maintain a safe speed. - Watch
field edges, bunds, drains, slopes, and muddy areas. - Stop immediately
if an unusual noise, vibration, smell, leak, or overheating condition
occurs.

Before maintenance: - Park safely. - Lower raised implements to the
ground. - Turn off the engine. - Remove the ignition key where
applicable. - Disengage PTO and other power sources. - Relieve hydraulic
pressure where required. - Wait for all moving parts to stop. - Use
appropriate tools and PPE.

Chemical application: - Correctly identify the crop problem. - Use only
legally approved products. - Follow the product label exactly. - Use the
recommended dose and application method. - Wear the specified PPE. -
Avoid spraying people, animals, food, homes, and waterways. - Keep
chemicals locked away from children and food. - Wash equipment and PPE
according to the label instructions.

Emergency response: - Know how to stop the machine quickly. - Keep a
first-aid kit available. - Keep emergency contact information
accessible. - For chemical exposure, follow the product label’s
first-aid instructions and seek medical assistance when required. - For
serious machinery injuries, stop the machine, provide first aid only
when safe, and obtain emergency medical assistance.

MACHINE SELECTION BY PADDY FARMING STAGE

Land preparation: - Tractor - Power tiller - Plough - Rotavator -
Puddler - Harrow - Land leveler

Planting: - Seed drill/direct seeder - Rice transplanter

Water management: - Water pump - Tractor/PTO pump

Fertilizer application: - Fertilizer broadcaster/spreader - Knapsack
sprayer for approved foliar products

Weed management: - Mechanical weeder - Knapsack sprayer for approved
herbicides

Crop protection: - Knapsack sprayer - Tractor-mounted sprayer -
Agricultural drone where legally permitted and appropriate

Harvesting: - Rice reaper - Combine harvester

Post-harvest: - Rice thresher - Winnower/grain cleaner - Grain dryer -
Bagging/weighing machine - Rice milling machine

Transport: - Tractor trailer

QUICK MACHINE SELECTION GUIDE

Question: “I need to prepare a flooded paddy field.” Use: - Puddler and
suitable tractor/power tiller. Important: - Ensure safe water and soil
conditions and avoid unstable field edges.

Question: “I need to level my paddy field.” Use: - Land leveler or laser
land leveler. Important: - Proper leveling can help improve water
distribution.

Question: “I want to plant rice seedlings quickly.” Use: - Rice
transplanter. Important: - Prepare suitable seedling mats/trays and a
properly prepared field.

Question: “I want to directly sow rice.” Use: - Direct seeder/seed
drill. Important: - Calibrate seed rate and prepare the field according
to the direct-seeding method.

Question: “The rice is ready to harvest.” Use: - Reaper or combine
harvester. Important: - Confirm crop maturity and check machine
settings.

Question: “I need to separate rice grain from harvested plants.” Use: -
Rice thresher. Important: - Never put hands into the feeding/threshing
mechanism.

Question: “I need to clean harvested grain.” Use: - Winnower or grain
cleaner. Important: - Control dust and keep hands away from moving
parts.

Question: “The paddy is too wet after harvest.” Use: - Grain dryer,
where available. Important: - Use the correct drying temperature and
monitor grain moisture.

Question: “I need to pump water into/out of the field.” Use: - Water
pump. Important: - Follow electrical safety rules for electric pumps and
keep fuel away from hot engines.

Question: “I need to spray a crop protection product.” Use: - Knapsack
sprayer, tractor-mounted sprayer, or approved agricultural drone.
Important: - Correctly identify the problem and follow the product label
and PPE requirements.

Question: “I need to transport harvested paddy.” Use: - Tractor trailer.
Important: - Do not exceed rated capacity and secure the load.
