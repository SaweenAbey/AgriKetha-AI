Chilli crop guide

Planting: Use healthy seedlings and plant in well-drained soil with good sunlight. Leave enough spacing for air movement.

Water: Water when the topsoil begins to dry. Avoid waterlogging and wet leaves for long periods.

Fertilizer: Add mature compost before planting. Apply fertilizer in small split doses during vegetative growth and flowering.

Pests: Check leaves regularly for aphids, thrips, and mites. Remove badly affected plant parts and use locally approved control methods when needed.

Diseases: Good drainage, clean tools, crop rotation, and removal of infected leaves help reduce disease spread.

Harvest: Pick mature green or red chillies carefully to avoid damaging branches.
