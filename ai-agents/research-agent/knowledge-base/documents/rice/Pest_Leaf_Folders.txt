Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Rice Leaffolder

Scientific group: Cnaphalocrocis medinalis and related species

Damage / symptoms: - Larvae fold or roll leaves and feed inside the
folded leaf. - White or transparent feeding areas may appear. - Severe
damage reduces photosynthetic leaf area.

Management: 1. Inspect folded leaves and larval presence. 2. Early
damage is often tolerated by rice because plants can compensate. 3.
Avoid routine insecticide spraying during early crop stages unless
monitoring indicates a real need. 4. Preserve parasitoids, predators,
and other natural enemies. 5. Use insecticide only when economically
justified and locally recommended.

Recovery: - Healthy rice can produce new leaves after moderate early
damage. - Maintain good crop nutrition and water management.
