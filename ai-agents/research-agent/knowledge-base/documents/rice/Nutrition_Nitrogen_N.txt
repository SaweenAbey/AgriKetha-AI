Crop: Rice
Category: Nutrition

Nutrition: Nitrogen (N) Deficiency

Role: - Nitrogen is essential for vegetative growth, chlorophyll
formation, and yield.

Typical symptoms: - Older leaves become yellowish or pale green first. -
Plants may be stunted. - Reduced tillering. - Overall crop growth is
weak.

Possible causes: - Low soil nitrogen. - Insufficient fertilizer. -
Nutrient losses. - Poor timing or placement of nitrogen fertilizer.

Management / recovery: 1. Confirm the diagnosis because yellowing can
have several causes. 2. Follow a site-specific or locally recommended
nitrogen management plan. 3. Apply nitrogen at the recommended rate and
timing rather than applying a large amount at once. 4. Split nitrogen
applications where recommended. 5. Consider soil and crop history. 6.
Avoid over-application because excessive nitrogen can increase
susceptibility to some pests and diseases.

Warning: - Do not tell a farmer to apply a fixed amount of urea without
knowing field size, soil condition, crop stage, previous fertilizer use,
and local recommendation.
