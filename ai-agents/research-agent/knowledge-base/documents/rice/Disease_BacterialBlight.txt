Crop: Rice
Category: Disease

Disease: Bacterial Blight

Scientific cause: Xanthomonas oryzae pv. oryzae

Identification / symptoms: - Water-soaked to yellowish lesions commonly
begin near leaf margins or tips. - Lesions enlarge and may become pale
yellow to whitish. - Severely affected leaves dry from the tip
downward. - Young plants can wilt severely. - The disease is favored by
stormy weather, strong winds, high humidity, and excessive nitrogen.

Effects: - Reduces healthy leaf area and photosynthesis. - Severe
infection can reduce grain filling and yield.

Management: 1. Use resistant or locally recommended varieties where
available. 2. Avoid excessive nitrogen fertilizer. 3. Maintain balanced
plant nutrition. 4. Keep fields and irrigation channels clean and manage
volunteer rice. 5. Avoid practices that unnecessarily injure plants. 6.
Monitor fields regularly, especially after storms and during humid
conditions. 7. Seek local extension advice before applying bactericides
because chemical control can be limited and product registration varies
by country.

Recovery: - Remove or manage heavily infected plants when practical. -
Maintain balanced nutrition and appropriate water management. - Protect
remaining healthy plants from additional stress. - Affected leaf tissue
cannot be restored; the goal is to protect new growth and reduce spread.
