Crop: Rice
Category: Disease

Disease: Blast

Scientific cause: Magnaporthe oryzae

Identification / symptoms: - Leaf lesions are often spindle- or
diamond-shaped with pointed ends. - Lesions can enlarge and join
together. - Collar or neck infections can cause serious damage to
panicles. - Blast can be confused with brown spot, so diagnosis should
consider lesion shape and plant stage.

Effects: - Severe leaf blast can reduce leaf area available for grain
filling. - Neck or panicle blast can cause poor grain development and
major yield loss.

Management: 1. Plant resistant varieties recommended for the local area.
2. Avoid excessive nitrogen. 3. Split nitrogen applications instead of
applying excessive nitrogen at once. 4. Maintain suitable water
management. 5. Use balanced nutrition. 6. Use clean seed and good field
sanitation. 7. Where locally recommended and legally registered,
fungicides such as appropriate triazole or strobilurin products may be
used according to the label and local extension guidance. 8. Monitor
especially around heading when neck blast risk is important.

Recovery: - Existing dead lesions will not become healthy again. -
Protect newly emerging leaves and panicles. - Correct excessive nitrogen
or other management problems. - Severe neck blast should be reported to
an agricultural officer because yield protection may require timely
intervention.
