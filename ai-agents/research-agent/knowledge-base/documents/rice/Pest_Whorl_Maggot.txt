Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Whorl Maggot

Scientific name commonly associated with rice whorl maggot: Hydrellia
spp.; species vary by region.

Damage / symptoms: - Maggots feed on young tissue inside the whorl. -
Young leaves may show yellowish-white longitudinal damage. - Leaves may
shrivel or show holes. - Severe damage can delay maturity.

Management: 1. Early crop damage is often tolerated and rice may
compensate. 2. Remove alternate hosts where practical. 3. Adjust
planting time according to local recommendations. 4. Maintain balanced
potassium and overall nutrient management. 5. Encourage natural enemies.
6. Avoid unnecessary early insecticide applications.

Recovery: - New leaves can develop after early damage. - Severe or
persistent damage should be assessed by an agricultural officer.
