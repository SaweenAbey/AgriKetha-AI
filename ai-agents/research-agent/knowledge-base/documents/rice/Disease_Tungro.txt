Crop: Rice
Category: Disease

Disease: Tungro

Cause: - Rice tungro disease is caused by a combination of rice tungro
viruses. - Green leafhoppers are important vectors.

Identification / symptoms: - Yellow to orange-yellow leaves. - Stunted
plants. - Reduced tillering. - Symptoms can resemble nitrogen
deficiency, zinc deficiency, water stress, or pest damage.

Effects: - Severe early infection can strongly reduce plant growth and
yield.

Important fact: - An already infected rice plant cannot be cured of
tungro.

Management: 1. Plant tungro- or leafhopper-resistant varieties where
locally available. 2. Synchronize planting with neighboring farms when
practical. 3. Avoid planting at times of high green leafhopper abundance
where local guidance supports this. 4. Remove severely infected plants
when appropriate. 5. Plow infected stubble after harvest according to
local recommendations. 6. Manage weeds and volunteer rice that may
support vectors. 7. Do not rely on insecticide alone because green
leafhoppers can move rapidly between fields.

Recovery: - Individual infected plants cannot be cured. - Protect the
rest of the crop by reducing vector pressure and removing infection
sources. - Replanting decisions should be made with local agricultural
advice when infection is severe.
