Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Stem Borer

Examples: - Yellow stem borer - Striped stem borer - Other rice stem
borers

Damage / symptoms: - Larvae bore into stems. - Vegetative-stage damage
causes deadhearts. - Reproductive-stage damage can cause whiteheads. -
Frass or larval material may be found inside damaged stems.

Management: 1. Use resistant or locally suitable varieties. 2. Handpick
and destroy egg masses where practical. 3. Synchronize planting. 4.
Remove volunteer rice and manage crop residues. 5. Harvest and manage
stubble appropriately. 6. Encourage natural enemies and biological
control. 7. Apply nitrogen according to recommended timing and rate. 8.
Use insecticide only when justified by monitoring and local
recommendations.

Recovery: - Dead tillers generally will not recover. - Protect
unaffected tillers and panicles. - Improve field sanitation for the next
crop.
