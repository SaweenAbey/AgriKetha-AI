Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Green Leafhopper (GLH)

Damage / symptoms: - Adults and nymphs suck sap from leaves. - Heavy
feeding can weaken plants. - Important vector of tungro disease.

Management: 1. Use resistant varieties where available. 2. Synchronize
planting where practical. 3. Remove volunteer rice and manage weeds that
can maintain vector populations. 4. Monitor populations. 5. Avoid
unnecessary broad-spectrum insecticides that kill natural enemies. 6.
Manage tungro by reducing vector movement and using resistant varieties.

Recovery: - Reduce leafhopper pressure and protect young plants. - If
tungro has already infected plants, the virus cannot be cured; protect
the remaining crop.
