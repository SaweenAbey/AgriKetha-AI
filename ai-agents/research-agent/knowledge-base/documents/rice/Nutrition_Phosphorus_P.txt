Crop: Rice
Category: Nutrition

Nutrition: Phosphorus (P) Deficiency

Role: - Important for root development, energy transfer, early growth,
and reproductive development.

Typical symptoms: - Stunted growth. - Dark green foliage may occur. -
Reduced tillering. - Delayed crop development.

Management / recovery: 1. Confirm deficiency using soil/plant assessment
where possible. 2. Apply phosphorus according to soil test and local
rice fertilizer recommendations. 3. Correct soil conditions that reduce
phosphorus availability when applicable. 4. Avoid unnecessary
over-application.
