Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Rice Bug

Common name: Rice bug / rice stink bug Damage / symptoms: - Adults and
nymphs suck developing grains. - Damaged grains may become discolored,
shriveled, or poorly filled. - Damage is particularly important around
flowering and grain development.

Management: 1. Monitor fields during flowering and grain filling. 2.
Remove or manage weeds that provide alternate habitat when practical. 3.
Maintain field sanitation. 4. Use locally recommended control only when
monitoring indicates an economically important infestation. 5. Protect
natural enemies.

Recovery: - Damaged grains cannot be restored. - The goal is to protect
remaining developing grains.
