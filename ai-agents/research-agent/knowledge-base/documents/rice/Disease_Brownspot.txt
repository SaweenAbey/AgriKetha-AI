Crop: Rice
Category: Disease

Disease: Brown Spot

Scientific cause: Bipolaris oryzae (commonly associated with brown spot)

Identification / symptoms: - Small circular to oval brown lesions occur
on leaves. - Mature lesions may have a darker margin and lighter
center. - Severe infection can affect grains. - Brown spot can be
confused with blast.

Common contributing factors: - Nutrient stress, particularly poor
overall crop nutrition. - Drought or water stress. - Poor soil
fertility. - Weak or stressed plants.

Effects: - Reduced photosynthesis. - Reduced grain quality and yield
under severe disease pressure.

Management: 1. Maintain balanced soil fertility. 2. Correct confirmed
nutrient deficiencies. 3. Avoid prolonged water stress. 4. Use healthy
seed. 5. Remove or manage infected crop residues where appropriate. 6.
Improve field sanitation. 7. Use locally recommended fungicide options
only when needed and according to local labels.

Recovery: - Correct underlying nutrient or water stress. - Protect new
growth. - Severely damaged leaves will not recover, but improved
management can reduce new infection.
