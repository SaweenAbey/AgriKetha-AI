Crop: Rice
Category: General Recovery

When a farmer asks “How can I recover my rice crop?”:

1.  Identify the likely problem first.
2.  Check crop age and growth stage.
3.  Inspect several plants rather than one plant.
4.  Look for patterns across the field.
5.  Check recent fertilizer use and water management.
6.  Check for insects, disease lesions, and nutrient symptoms.
7.  Correct the underlying cause.
8.  Protect healthy/new growth.
9.  Avoid unnecessary pesticide applications.
10. Use resistant varieties and good field sanitation for future crops.
11. If diagnosis is uncertain or damage is severe, recommend
    professional confirmation.
