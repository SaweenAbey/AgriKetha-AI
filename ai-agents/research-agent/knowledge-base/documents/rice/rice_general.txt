Rice crop guide

Planting: Use healthy seed and prepare a level field. Maintain suitable spacing and remove weeds early.

Water: Keep enough water for establishment, but avoid unnecessary continuous flooding. Drain the field before harvest.

Fertilizer: Apply nutrients according to soil-test results. Nitrogen is commonly divided into applications during crop growth.

Pests: Inspect plants for stem borers, leaf folders, and planthoppers. Use integrated pest management and avoid unnecessary spraying.

Diseases: Use clean seed, balanced fertilizer, proper spacing, and resistant varieties where available to reduce disease risk.

Harvest: Harvest when most grains are mature and dry the grain properly after threshing.
