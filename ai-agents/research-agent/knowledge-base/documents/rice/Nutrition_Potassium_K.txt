Crop: Rice
Category: Nutrition

Nutrition: Potassium (K) Deficiency

Role: - Supports water regulation, plant strength, stress tolerance, and
disease/pest resilience.

Possible symptoms: - Older leaves may develop yellowing or browning,
especially toward margins or tips. - Weak growth and reduced stress
tolerance can occur. - Symptoms vary with variety and soil conditions.

Management / recovery: 1. Confirm potassium deficiency rather than
diagnosing only from leaf color. 2. Apply potassium according to soil
testing or locally recommended rice nutrient management. 3. Maintain
balanced N-P-K nutrition. 4. Avoid excessive nitrogen without adequate
potassium because nutrient imbalance can increase crop problems.
