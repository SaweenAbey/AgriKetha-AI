Crop: Rice
Category: Pest

Pest: Brown Planthopper (BPH)

Common name: Brown planthopper Scientific group: Nilaparvata lugens

Damage / symptoms: - Nymphs and adults suck plant sap. - Heavy
infestation causes hopper burn: leaves dry and plants turn brown. -
Plants may lodge. - BPH can transmit grassy stunt and ragged stunt
viruses.

Management: 1. Monitor fields regularly. 2. Use locally recommended
resistant varieties where available. 3. Avoid excessive nitrogen. 4.
Avoid unnecessary early-season insecticide applications. 5. Protect
natural enemies such as spiders. 6. Drain or manage water according to
local recommendations when BPH pressure is high. 7. If insecticide is
justified by monitoring and threshold information, use a locally
registered selective product according to its label.

Recovery: - Reduce pest pressure quickly when economic thresholds are
exceeded. - Allow surviving plants to recover through good water and
nutrient management. - Severely hopper-burned plants may not recover
fully.
