Crop: Tomato
Category: General Recovery

When a farmer asks how to recover a tomato plant:

1.  Identify the likely disease/pest first.
2.  Check whether the problem is fungal, bacterial, viral,
    insect-related, or environmental.
3.  Inspect multiple leaves and plants.
4.  Check the underside of leaves for mites and insects.
5.  Check irrigation, humidity, drainage, temperature, and recent
    weather.
6.  Review fertilizer use and nutrient balance.
7.  Remove or isolate severely infected plant material when appropriate.
8.  Improve airflow and reduce unnecessary leaf wetness.
9.  Use resistant varieties for future planting where available.
10. Use chemical control only when justified and according to local
    label requirements.
11. For suspected viral disease, focus on prevention and vector
    management rather than attempting a “cure”.
12. If diagnosis is uncertain or the crop is severely affected,
    recommend confirmation by an agricultural extension officer or plant
    pathologist.
