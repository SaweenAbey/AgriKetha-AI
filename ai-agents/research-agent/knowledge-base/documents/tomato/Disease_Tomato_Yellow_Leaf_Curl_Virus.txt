10. TOMATO
YELLOW LEAF CURL VIRUS (TYLCV)

Scientific cause: Tomato yellow leaf curl virus, transmitted by the
whitefly Bemisia tabaci.

Identification / symptoms: - Upward curling and yellowing of leaflet
margins. - Stunted, bushy growth with small, pale leaves. - Flower drop
and greatly reduced fruit set on infected plants.

Effects: - Yield loss can be severe, especially when plants are infected
young. - The virus spreads rapidly wherever whitefly populations are
high.

Management: 1. Control whitefly populations using recommended
insecticides or biological controls. 2. Use resistant or tolerant
varieties where available. 3. Remove and destroy infected plants early
to reduce virus source. 4. Use reflective mulches or fine exclusion
netting to deter whiteflies. 5. Avoid planting new seedlings next to
heavily infected fields.

Recovery: - Infected plants do not recover; prevention through whitefly
control is the main defense. - Monitor new growth closely after
transplanting.
