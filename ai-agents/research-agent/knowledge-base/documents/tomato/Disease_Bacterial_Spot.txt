Crop: Tomato
Category: Disease

1.
BACTERIAL SPOT

Scientific cause: Xanthomonas spp. (X. euvesicatoria, X. vesicatoria, X. perforans).

Identification / symptoms: - Small, dark, water-soaked spots on leaves that
turn brown to black with a yellow halo. - Spots may merge and cause leaf
tearing or drop. - Raised, scabby, rough spots appear on green fruit.

Effects: - Heavy leaf loss reduces plant vigor and sunscald risk on fruit.
- Blemished fruit lowers market quality even when yield is not badly
affected.

Management: 1. Use certified disease-free seed and transplants. 2. Avoid
overhead irrigation and working in wet foliage. 3. Rotate away from
tomato and pepper for at least one season. 4. Apply copper-based
bactericides preventively where locally approved. 5. Remove and destroy
crop debris after harvest.

Recovery: - Spotted leaves will not clear up; focus on protecting new
growth. - Improve airflow and reduce leaf wetness duration. - Severe,
spreading outbreaks should be confirmed by an extension officer.
