7. SPIDER
MITES (TWO-SPOTTED SPIDER MITE)

Scientific cause: Tetranychus urticae.

Identification / symptoms: - Fine yellow or white stippling/speckling on
leaves, often starting on the underside. - Leaves can turn bronze or
yellow and dry out under heavy infestation. - Fine webbing may be visible
on the underside of leaves or between stems in severe cases. - Mites are
tiny and often easier to detect with a hand lens than with the naked
eye.

Effects: - Reduced photosynthesis and leaf drop under heavy infestation,
lowering yield and plant vigor. - Populations build up fastest in hot,
dry, dusty conditions.

Management: 1. Increase humidity and reduce plant dust where practical.
2. Use insecticidal soap or labelled miticides for heavy infestations.
3. Encourage natural predators such as predatory mites. 4. Remove
heavily infested leaves. 5. Avoid broad-spectrum insecticides that kill
natural mite predators.

Recovery: - Damaged leaves will not regreen; focus on stopping further
spread and protecting new growth. - Recheck leaf undersides regularly
after treatment.
