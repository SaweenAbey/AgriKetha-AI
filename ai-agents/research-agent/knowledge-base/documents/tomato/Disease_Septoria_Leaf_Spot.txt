Crop: Tomato
Category: Disease

6. SEPTORIA
LEAF SPOT

Scientific cause: Septoria lycopersici.

Identification / symptoms: - Small, circular spots with dark brown
margins and light gray or tan centers, usually starting on older, lower
leaves. - Tiny black specks (pycnidia) are often visible in the center
of spots. - Spots can be numerous and merge, causing yellowing and leaf
drop.

Effects: - Progressive lower-leaf loss reduces plant vigor and can
expose fruit to sunscald if defoliation is severe.

Management: 1. Remove and destroy infected lower leaves as symptoms
appear. 2. Mulch around plants to reduce soil splash. 3. Rotate crops
away from tomato for at least one season. 4. Avoid overhead irrigation
and working with wet foliage. 5. Apply labelled fungicides where locally
approved for persistent outbreaks.

Recovery: - Existing spots will not clear; protect new upper growth by
controlling humidity and leaf wetness.
