3. HEALTHY
TOMATO

Identification: - Leaves are uniformly green with no spots, mold,
curling, mottling, or wilting. - No visible pest damage, webbing, or
scabby lesions on fruit or stems. - Growth is even and vigorous for the
plant's stage.

Notes: - This class is the baseline reference used to confirm that a
plant shows no signs of the diseases or pests covered in this knowledge
base. - A healthy classification does not rule out early-stage or
symptomless problems, so continue routine monitoring of leaf undersides
and new growth.

Recommended care: 1. Maintain consistent watering and avoid prolonged
leaf wetness. 2. Keep balanced fertilization based on plant stage.
3. Continue regular visual inspection, especially after wet or humid
weather.
