Crop: Tomato
Category: Disease

8. TARGET
SPOT

Scientific cause: Corynespora cassiicola.

Identification / symptoms: - Brown lesions with faint concentric rings,
similar to early blight but often larger and less defined. - Spots can
appear on leaves, stems, and fruit. - Lesions may have a slightly sunken
center and can merge to form large necrotic patches.

Effects: - Significant leaf loss under warm, humid conditions can reduce
yield and expose fruit to sun damage. - Fruit lesions reduce
marketability.

Management: 1. Rotate crops and avoid continuous tomato planting.
2. Remove and destroy crop debris after harvest. 3. Improve airflow
through spacing and pruning. 4. Avoid prolonged leaf wetness from
overhead irrigation. 5. Apply labelled fungicides in rotation where the
disease is severe or persistent.

Recovery: - Focus on protecting healthy foliage; existing lesions will
not heal. - Reduce humidity and leaf wetness where practical.
