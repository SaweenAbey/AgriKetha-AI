Crop: Tomato
Category: Disease

5. LEAF
MOLD

Scientific cause: Passalora fulva (syn. Fulvia fulva / Cladosporium
fulvum).

Identification / symptoms: - Pale green to yellow spots on the upper
leaf surface. - Olive-green to grayish-purple velvety mold growth on the
corresponding underside of the leaf. - Most common in greenhouses or
areas with high humidity and poor air circulation.

Effects: - Reduced photosynthesis from leaf yellowing and early leaf
drop, which can lower yield in enclosed or densely planted conditions.

Management: 1. Improve ventilation and reduce humidity around plants.
2. Increase plant spacing and prune for better airflow. 3. Avoid
overhead watering, especially late in the day. 4. Use resistant
varieties where available. 5. Apply labelled fungicides if the disease
is severe or persistent.

Recovery: - Improving airflow and humidity control usually stops further
spread. - Remove heavily affected leaves to reduce spore load.
