9. TOMATO
MOSAIC VIRUS

Scientific cause: Tomato mosaic virus (ToMV).

Identification / symptoms: - Light and dark green mottled (mosaic)
pattern on leaves. - Leaf distortion, curling, or fern-like narrowing in
severe cases. - Stunted growth and reduced fruit set or quality.

Effects: - No direct cure exists; yield and fruit quality losses depend
on infection timing and severity. - The virus is very stable and can
persist in plant debris, soil, and on tools or hands for a long time.

Management: 1. Remove and destroy infected plants; there is no chemical
cure. 2. Disinfect tools and wash hands between handling plants,
especially after contact with tobacco products, which can carry the
virus. 3. Use certified virus-free seed and resistant varieties where
available. 4. Avoid working in the crop when plants are wet.

Recovery: - Infected plants do not recover; focus on preventing spread
to healthy plants. - Practice strict sanitation for future plantings.
