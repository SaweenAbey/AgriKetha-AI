Tomato crop guide

Planting: Transplant healthy seedlings into fertile, well-drained soil with full sunlight. Provide support as plants grow.

Water: Water deeply at the base and keep moisture consistent. Avoid frequent wetting of leaves and avoid waterlogging.

Fertilizer: Add compost before planting and apply balanced fertilizer in stages. Increase attention to potassium during fruit development.

Pests: Monitor for whiteflies, aphids, fruit borers, and mites. Remove damaged fruit and use locally approved integrated control methods.

Diseases: Remove infected leaves, improve air circulation, rotate crops, and avoid working with wet plants.

Harvest: Pick tomatoes at the maturity stage needed for transport or immediate use.
